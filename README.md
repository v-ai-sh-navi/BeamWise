# BeamWise

BeamWise is a React-based web application that automates the design of reinforced concrete (RCC) beams according to **IS 456:2000**. Developed by converting an Excel-based beam design workflow into an interactive web application, BeamWise makes structural design calculations faster, more intuitive, and easier to visualize.

## Features

- **Beam Geometry Design** – Define beam dimensions and design parameters.
- **Load Calculations** – Calculate dead loads and live loads.
- **Structural Analysis** – Calculate ultimate loads, bending moments, and shear forces.
- **Depth Recommendation** – Determine effective depth and recommend overall beam depth.
- **Flexural Design** – Perform flexural design calculations for RCC beams.
- **Reinforcement Design** – Calculate required steel area and reinforcement bar arrangements.
- **Shear Design** – Determine shear reinforcement requirements and stirrup spacing.
- **Safety Checks** – Evaluate flexural and shear safety requirements.
- **Deflection Check** – Evaluate beam deflection against applicable serviceability limits.
- **Crack Width Check** – Assess crack width for serviceability requirements.
- **Beam Cross-Section Visualization** – Visualize the beam cross-section and reinforcement arrangement.
- **PDF Report Generation** – Generate downloadable PDF reports of beam design calculations and results.
- **Interactive User Interface** – Explore design inputs, calculations, and results through a component-based React interface.
